VEHICLE
SPECIFICATION
GUIDE

Understand the key specifications used in vehicle valuation and how they influence estimated market value.

Core Specifications

WHAT WE
MEASURE

01

Engine Size

Total engine displacement of the vehicle. Larger engines generally provide more power and may increase vehicle value.

02

Curb Weight

Weight of the vehicle without passengers or cargo. Heavier vehicles are often associated with premium segments.

03

Horsepower

Engine power output. Higher horsepower usually improves performance and market appeal.

04

Car Width

Overall width of the vehicle body. Wider vehicles often provide more cabin comfort and stability.

05

Car Length

Distance from front bumper to rear bumper. Larger vehicles may offer increased interior space.

06

Wheelbase

Distance between front and rear wheels. Longer wheelbases generally improve ride comfort.

07

Bore Ratio

Diameter of engine cylinders which influences engine performance characteristics.

08

City MPG

Fuel efficiency during city driving. Higher MPG means lower fuel consumption.

09

Highway MPG

Fuel efficiency achieved on highways and long-distance travel.

Drive Configuration

DRIVE WHEEL
TYPES

1

Front Wheel Drive

FWD

Power is delivered to the front wheels. Provides good traction and fuel efficiency for everyday driving.

2

Rear Wheel Drive

RWD

Power is delivered to the rear wheels. Offers better balance and sporty driving performance.

0

Four Wheel Drive

4WD

Power is delivered to all four wheels. Improves traction and control in challenging conditions.

Fuel Technology

FUEL SYSTEM
TYPES

0

1BBL

Single-barrel carburetor used in older economy vehicles.

1

2BBL

Two-barrel carburetor offering improved fuel-air mixing.

2

4BBL

High-performance four-barrel carburetor used in powerful engines.

3

IDI

Indirect diesel injection system for diesel-powered vehicles.

4

MFI

Mechanical Fuel Injection system with precise fuel delivery.

5

MPFI

Multi-Point Fuel Injection. One injector per cylinder for efficiency.

6

SPDI

Single Point Direct Injection fuel delivery technology.

7

SPFI

Single Point Fuel Injection system used in compact vehicles.
